Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Subtl is developed and published by Anton Smedberg, responsible for the personal information handled for Subtl support. Contact anton@smedberg.eu with privacy questions or requests.

This policy covers the Subtl iPhone and iPad app, its support website and support email. Videos, audio and captions are processed on your device. Subtl has no accounts, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK, and does not upload your projects to a server operated by the developer.

Videos, captions and Apple models

You choose videos through the system Photos or Files picker. Subtl keeps a working copy, transcript and caption settings locally so you can reopen and edit your project. It does not need general Photos-library access to use the videos you select with the system picker.

Speech recognition and optional writing, image-description and video-enhancement features use supported Apple frameworks and models on your device. Availability depends on the device, system version, language and settings. Apple may need to download models. Subtl does not send your content to a cloud AI fallback when a model is unavailable.

A file you choose from iCloud or another provider may first be downloaded by that provider. Its privacy practices apply to that service.

Exports, sharing and backups

Subtl requests permission to add a video to Photos when you choose to save an export there. Manage app permissions in your device’s Settings.

Copy places the text you choose on the system clipboard. Sharing sends the chosen output to the app or destination you select. A portable .subtlproject backup contains your project video, transcript and caption settings; Subtl first prepares a temporary copy, then you choose where to save it in Files. The receiving app or storage provider has its own privacy practices.

Local storage and deletion

Project copies and preferences stay in Subtl’s local storage. Deleting a project moves it to Recently Deleted for seven days. You can use Undo immediately, restore it from Settings → Storage and backups during that period, or choose Delete permanently. Expired copies are removed when Subtl next loads or refreshes the library. Video storage is released after the deletion has been saved successfully.

Subtl does not delete your original video in Photos or Files, saved exports or backups stored elsewhere. Manage those copies in their app or service. Restoring a project backup creates a separate project.

Import, preview and export use temporary files. Abandoned subtitle and project-backup sharing copies expire after 24 hours and are removed on a later launch; the system may clear temporary storage sooner.

Your device’s backup settings may include Subtl’s app data. Deleting a local project does not immediately remove it from an existing device backup. Manage those backups through Apple or your chosen provider.

Diagnostics and support email

Subtl records operational diagnostics through Apple’s system logging tools. It does not automatically send those logs to a developer-operated service. Apple’s diagnostics and anything you choose to share depend on your system settings and Apple’s practices.

If you email support, Anton Smedberg processes your email address, name if provided, message and any attachments to answer you and investigate the reported issue. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining Subtl. Please start with a description of the problem; private videos, transcripts and passwords are not needed for an initial report.

Incoming email for anton@smedberg.eu is delivered through one.com. Its mail service processes the correspondence for email delivery. Email is not published on the support website or used for advertising.

Routine support correspondence is deleted within 12 months after the request is resolved, or sooner when it is no longer needed. Unnecessary attachments are removed sooner. Information needed for an unresolved dispute or legal obligation may be kept for that purpose until it is no longer required. Service-provider backup copies follow that provider’s retention arrangements.

The support website

This website adds no analytics, advertising, cookies or contact forms. Email links open your own mail app.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and records visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub may process technical information internationally, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The developer does not use these visits to build advertising profiles.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information held for support, restrict its use, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or receive a portable copy where applicable. Email anton@smedberg.eu; requests will be handled within the applicable legal time limit, normally one month under the GDPR. Limited information may be requested to verify that the request is yours.

The developer cannot remotely access your local projects. You control them through the app and your device or backup settings.

You may also complain to your local data-protection authority, including Sweden’s Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes to this policy

This page will show the effective date of any updated policy. Material changes to how Subtl handles your information will be reflected in the app and its App Store privacy disclosures.
