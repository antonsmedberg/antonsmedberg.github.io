Speech recognition or an AI tool isn’t available

Subtl captions speech in Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Icelandic, Faroese, English, Spanish, German, French and Thai, wherever iOS supports them on your iPhone. Some tools depend on your device, language, iOS version and an Apple model download. Follow the availability message in Subtl and let any required download finish.

You can still edit existing captions and export your video without the optional Apple Intelligence tools. Search finds clip names and spoken words without them too.

My captions need a correction

In Studio, open Edit words to correct the text. From there, Word timing splits a word, merges it with the next word in the same caption, removes it with Remove word, or adjusts when it starts and ends.

Save, preview the result and use Undo if you need to restore the previous edit.

I can’t save an export to Photos

Check Subtl’s Photos permission in the device’s Settings and make sure enough storage is available. Follow the error shown in the app before retrying.

After a successful save, the completion screen offers sharing. Keep the source video until you’ve checked the exported picture, captions and audio.

The app freezes, closes or my phone gets hot

If possible, cancel the current operation and keep Subtl open until any saving notice has cleared. Note the screen and action where the issue happened. Check available storage and whether the problem happens with one clip or several.

If the app is unresponsive, reopen it and check your saved clips. Don’t delete the app as a troubleshooting step: that can remove its local clips. Contact support with the details below; you don’t need to send your private video to start a report.

How do I back up or restore a clip?

Open Settings → Storage and backups, choose a clip and save a .subtlproject file to Files. The package includes the video, transcript and caption settings.

Restore a backup from the same screen. It creates a separate clip. Keep backups in a trusted location, and preserve the original backup until you’ve checked the restored clip.

Can I recover a deleted clip?

Use Undo immediately after deleting, or open Settings → Storage and backups → Recently deleted. Clips remain recoverable for seven days unless you delete them permanently sooner.

Expired clips are removed when Subtl opens and whenever you return to it. Original videos, exported videos and backups saved outside Subtl are managed separately.

Subtl says changes haven’t finished saving

Keep Subtl open, check available storage and use Retry in the storage notice. Avoid deleting the app or its files. If the notice continues, contact support and include its exact wording.

If a library can’t be loaded, retry storage before making changes. The app protects the unreadable catalog from being overwritten.

Where does my content go?

Subtl processes videos, audio and captions on your device. It doesn’t send that content to a developer-operated server. Apple models may need an initial download, and a video chosen from a cloud file provider may need to be downloaded by that provider.

Sharing and backups go to the destination you choose. The app also prepares temporary working files, including a temporary project-backup package. A finished export stays in Subtl’s temporary folder for an hour after it last changed, so sharing can finish, and is then removed. For privacy questions, email anton@smedberg.eu.
