DeviceMonitor

Grit Academy final project (Distinction), developed into my own app

May 2026–Present

  • A network monitor for iPhone and iPad that finds the devices on the local Wi-Fi network with TCP, ICMP and UDP probes and Bonjour, and charts latency, jitter and outages over time.
