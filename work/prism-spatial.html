Prism Spatial

Rendering research around Gaussian splats and spatial captures, with a custom Metal path when higher-level framework abstractions become the constraint.

Why I built it

The project started as a rendering experiment rather than a finished product. I wanted to understand how far the available spatial frameworks could take the idea and where a custom renderer became the clearer option.

Engineering approach

  • Import PLY-based Gaussian-splat data and keep the rendering path explicit.
  • Use a custom Metal renderer where lower-level projection and drawing control is useful.
  • Treat tilt-shift and colour grading as part of the presentation concept rather than unrelated effects.
  • Rescope when a framework abstraction becomes the constraint instead of forcing the whole project around it.

Current state

Prism Spatial is experimental work and is labelled that way. I include it because it shows graphics depth, framework evaluation and technical decision-making, not because I want it to look more finished than it is.
